## Source Context Added to the Proposal

The original proposal states:

- the website package was **$700 one time**;
- the current overall website investment is understood to be approximately **$1,000**, subject to confirmation against the payment record;
- the project was intentionally positioned as an affordable birthday gift and scalable foundation;
- the package mentioned a basic Events and Classes calendar with booking forms;
- the package mentioned newsletter signup, Contentful, bookings, Stripe foundations, analytics, and other features; and
- the original estimated timeline was four to six weeks.

Its strategic context was also broader than a list of pages. The website was meant to move WaistLess Foods beyond an Instagram-dependent presence, centralize recipes, services, events, and lifestyle content, reduce manual promotion and inquiries, and establish Chef Amber as an approachable Houston-based culinary brand. The revised Events scope should be presented as a practical continuation of that original “cook, book, or join” strategy, not as a new standalone platform.

This matters commercially. A $2,100 Events quote would exceed roughly twice the website investment to date and would appear disproportionate to both the original package price and the fact that an Events foundation was mentioned in the original scope. It could also create a disagreement about whether the basic feature had already been purchased.

The revised proposal therefore does three things:

1. acknowledges the original Events and newsletter foundation;
2. charges only for the newly clarified reusable Events pages and internal newsletter workflow; and
3. keeps custom registration, ticketing, automation, and administration outside the small add-on.

## Recommended Commercial Position

- **Small Events MVP:** $400 fixed.
- **Basic WaistLess-managed newsletter workflow:** $400 fixed.
- **Combined add-on:** $700 fixed.
- **Small retainer:** $300 per month for six months, with approximately five to six reserved hours per month.

These are relationship-based prices that fit the history of the project. They should not be described as general market rates.

## Scope Boundary

The Events MVP includes only:

- navigation;
- one listing page;
- one reusable detail template;
- a simple Contentful event model;
- one featured image and optional external video link;
- event date, time, location, description, and external registration URL;
- simple upcoming or past behavior;
- one initial event; and
- responsive verification plus one revision round.

Registration remains external. If Amber and John later request website-managed payments, capacity, waitlists, reminders, cancellations, or attendee administration, that is a separate project.

## Email Positioning

Do not call the email option “self-hosted email.” WaistLess Foods can own the subscriber list, campaign content, and protected workflow while a professional provider handles delivery and reputation.

The $400 email add-on is intentionally limited to one reusable newsletter layout, editable content, test sending, sending to active subscribers, current unsubscribe protection, and basic logging. It does not include a Mailchimp replacement, advanced segmentation, automation, detailed analytics, or unlimited campaign work.

## Retainer Positioning

The earlier internal draft proposed $750 per month for 12 months. That is now superseded by the smaller **$300 per month for six months** option in the client proposal. The reduced option is easier to relate to the original project value and creates a shorter decision horizon for Amber and John.

The retainer should begin only after the original website punch list is closed or the remaining original obligations are documented. Do not use the retainer to charge again for agreed original-scope corrections.
