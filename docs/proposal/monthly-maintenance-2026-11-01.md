# Monthly website maintenance — review draft

Prepared November 1, 2026. Internal draft for Zul's review; not sent or accepted.

## Proposed monthly option

**USD $300 per month**, month to month, with up to **five hours of website support per month**. The amount is based on the existing relationship-based $300 support option in the project notes, not an asserted market rate. The month-to-month term is a new proposed option, not a previously agreed term.

## Included work

- Investigate and correct website issues reported by Amber.
- Check booking, checkout, invoice, and newsletter workflows after relevant changes.
- Make small copy, image, layout, and configuration updates.
- Provide reasonable guidance on the existing administrator tools.
- Help prepare or troubleshoot an existing newsletter within the reserved hours when Amber supplies the content.

Amber retains independent access to the newsletter tools. Monthly support is not required for her to create or send an issue.

## Working arrangement

- Begin after the original launch obligations are complete, or after the remaining original work is explicitly documented separately.
- Acknowledge routine requests within two business days and agree on a delivery date after reviewing the issue. This is not an emergency or 24-hour support plan.
- Track time and provide a short monthly summary of changes and open issues.
- Do not exceed the five-hour allowance without a separate estimate and approval. Unused hours do not roll over under this proposed arrangement.
- Either party can end the arrangement before the next monthly renewal. The final start date and payment terms require agreement.

## Separate scope

New features, extensive redesigns, new integrations, social-media management, paid ads, blog writing, and full newsletter production are separately scoped. Hosting, domains, Google Workspace, Stripe fees, and other third-party charges remain outside the support fee.

Agreed original-scope fixes and pre-launch verification are not reclassified as chargeable retainer work. The new contract-signing workflow must have its implementation scope settled separately; it is not silently included in five monthly support hours.

## Suggested client wording

For ongoing website support after launch, I can offer an optional $300 monthly plan with up to five hours for maintenance, small updates, and help with the existing booking, payment, and newsletter tools. You would still be able to manage your content and send newsletters independently. Larger features and marketing work would be quoted separately, and I would check with you before any work exceeds the monthly allowance.
